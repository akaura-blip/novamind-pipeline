Campaign: 2025-10-18-creative-automation

Overview  
This week’s “AI in Creative Automation” campaign performed strongest with our creative audience and confirmed a growing interest in workflow simplicity across all segments.

Highlights  
- Creatives had the highest open and click rates, validating that storytelling and tone resonate when it focuses on reclaiming creative time.  
- Founders engaged well with ROI messaging but clicked less often—suggesting curiosity without full conviction.  
- Ops Managers trailed in both metrics; their content likely needs more specificity on integrations and reliability benefits.

Insights  
- Emotion and efficiency together drive engagement; creatives connect with feeling, founders with proof.  
- Shorter previews that frame an immediate benefit (“save 3 hours a day”) could lift opens across all segments.  
- Technical audiences need tangible outcomes, not slogans—clarity beats cleverness.

Next Experiments  
1) Add a single visual mockup or short demo clip to the creative segment newsletter.  
2) Test a ROI-focused subject line for founders: *“How one agency gained 6 extra billable hours a week.”*  
3) Rewrite the Ops version to open with a real integration example (Slack + Notion + Drive).
